How the 30 days were completed

The scheduled runs on 16 Sep. Nothing was started by hand.

  1. 07:30–07:42 UTC
    Daily load: 2,490 new addressesBoth base tables had changed; session summary now runs to 15 Sep. 6,345 rows inserted, 78 updated, all 14 checks passed. 3,410 addresses were queued for proxycheck.io (2,490 new, 610 flagged but unconfirmed, 304 with no answer, 6 where vendors disagreed).
  2. 07:42–07:49
    proxycheck.io: 49,512 queriesSent in 50 batches: 3,410 queued addresses first, then 12,769 from the last 30 days that other vendors called clean, then 33,325 with no answer (the rest of the 30 days first, then older addresses). 49,504 risk answers and 48,941 locations added; all 3,410 queued addresses answered.
  3. 07:48
    Reports rebuilt and checks passedResolved answers changed for 49,504 addresses. The coverage and accuracy reports were rebuilt; all 10 checks passed and the activity review found nothing outside the project.
  4. 07:49–07:52
    Unified tables refreshedBoth tables were replaced with copies checked against ipintel_curated: same row counts, 0 duplicates, and more than 200 sample addresses each with identical answers. The 10:00 fallback run found nothing new and refreshed them again with identical data.
  5. 10:35–10:42
    Location conflicts sorted and rebuiltNew location rules went live in the tables and the API: spelling-tolerant city matching, the statuses nearby (same state, within 25 km) and same state, and the vendor most often right in each kind of conflict chosen as the answer. 73,205 resolved rows changed; checks passed and the unified tables were refreshed again.
  6. 17 Sep onward
    Keeping the window enrichedAbout 400–1,500 new addresses a day are answered the next morning, well within the 50,000 queries. The rest of the budget works through older addresses.

Checked through the live API at 10:05 UTC: addresses from the last 30 days are answered from the tables with no new query, and an address not in the tables is sent to proxycheck.io once and then served from the saved answer.

Location agreement after the fix

Before the fix, 22,583 addresses in the window (53%) were labelled "vendors disagree" whenever two vendors named different cities. Most were the same state with a nearby or different city. They are now sorted by how far apart the answers are.

Where the data lives

  • ipintel_curated.ip_geo, ip_intelligence, ip_resolved: updated by the daily runs.
  • unified.ip_geo, unified.ip_intelligence: checked copies, refreshed after each successful proxycheck.io run. proxycheck.io answers went from 1 (11 Sep copy) to 77,710 (15 Sep) to 127,216 (16 Sep).
  • In the app: Reports → Enrichment Coverage and Location Accuracy, rebuilt after every run (API GET /coverage, GET /accuracy/geo).

Limits to know

  • Accuracy rests on 2,105 customer addresses; ipinfo.io and ipwho.is were tested on 1,050 of them.
  • For the last 30 days, 7,448 addresses (17%) still have location vendors naming different states. The answer shown comes from the vendor most often right in that situation. About 16,500 conflicts are waiting for a free-vendor tie-break: states differ in roughly 8 days, all in roughly 17.
  • All addresses, not just the last 30 days: 814,185 still undecided (848,403 on 15 Sep). At 50,000 a day the older backlog takes about 16–17 more days.
